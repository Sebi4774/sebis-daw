GOAT CHOIR - VST3 plugin for Windows (64-bit)          by Sebi Inc.
=====================================================================

WHAT'S IN THE ZIP
  Goat Choir.vst3   the plugin (a folder; keep it whole)
  Goat Choir.exe    the same thing as a stand-alone app, no DAW needed

INSTALL
  1. Copy the whole "Goat Choir.vst3" folder into
        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  2. FL Studio 20 or newer: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins.
     Goat Choir shows up under Generators.
     FL Studio 11 (VST2-only): add Carla-Rack to a channel, click Add Plugin,
     refresh, pick Goat Choir.
  Windows may warn that the file is from an unknown publisher (it is not
  code-signed yet): click More info > Run anyway.

PLAY IT
  * Keys C5 to D#6 (FL's middle C and up) play chord strips 1 to 16.
    Draw notes there in the piano roll, or play them on a MIDI keyboard.
  * Or set MIDI KEYS to "Sing my notes": the goats sing exactly the keys
    you hold, each goat in its own range.
  * Click a strip with the mouse to hear it (Latch on = it keeps singing).
  * Key, Scale and Colour (triads / 7ths / 9ths) pick the 16 chords.
  * Sing: Choir (full voicing), Unison, Solo (the selected goat).
    Style (8 ways to play a chord):
      Block   whole chord at once        Strum   rolled low to high
      Down    rolled high to low         Arp     up and back down, 1/8 notes
      Arp ↓   top to bottom, repeating   Broken  low, high, next, high
      Pulse   whole chord every 1/8      Bounce  bass on the beat, rest on the "and"
    The stepped styles (Arp to Bounce) follow the song's tempo, and the
    MIDI export writes them exactly as the goats sing them.
    Vowel: OO OH AH EH EE Mm.  Chop: a gate locked to the song's tempo.
  * Strum knob: how far apart the goats come in (Strum and Down). Turning it
    up from Block switches Style to Strum. Double-click resets it to 45%.
  * Motion: the choir's level follows a curve you draw, in time with the
    song. Pick a shape (Flat, Swell, Fade, Wave, Rise, Breathe, Pump, Gate,
    Stutter, Steps) or drag the points; click to add one, double-click to
    remove one. Global follows the song's bars, Retrig restarts on every
    chord. Length is 1/4 bar to 2 bars. Depth sets how deep it goes;
    Chance skips some cycles at random (the same way every time).
    Saved MIDI carries Motion as CC11 (expression).
  * Click a goat to select it: change its voice, mute it, change its scarf.
  * Words: type a line and the goats sing it, one syllable per chord (or
    one per step in Arp to Bounce), every goat on the same syllable. The
    word being sung lights up. "Words off" goes back to plain vowels and
    keeps your line. "Say" makes the selected goat speak the line.
    Saved MIDI carries the words as lyric events.

RECORD A PROGRESSION AND PUT IT IN FL
  1. Press Rec and play strips. If FL is playing, each chord's length
     follows the beat; otherwise each chord is 1 bar (click a chip to
     change it: 1, 2, 4 or 1/2 bar).
  2. Play loops it. While FL plays, the loop locks to the song position.
  3. Drag "Drag MIDI to FL" onto the playlist or the piano roll,
     or press Save MIDI (files go to Documents\Sebi Inc\Goat Choir).

MIDI OUT
  Goat Choir sends the notes the goats sing as MIDI, so another synth can
  double the choir. In FL 20+: set the plugin's MIDI output port in its
  wrapper settings and give the other synth the same input port.

The "Made with JUCE" logo shows for a few seconds when the window opens.
The voices are classic formant synthesis. No AI voices, ever.
